# Dynamic Chemicals Modernization Roadmap

## Phase 0 - Business and content audit

- Confirm ownership, goals, audience, service area, and conversion actions
- Verify all product claims, contact details, documents, and photography
- Remove starter content and identify missing trust/safety information
- Define product taxonomy, page inventory, and content ownership
- Choose the stack, CMS approach, analytics, hosting, and deployment process

## Phase 1 - Information architecture and design

- Map product discovery and inquiry journeys
- Design mobile-first navigation, product cards, detail pages, and search
- Establish visual identity, accessible color/typography tokens, and components
- Prototype inquiry and catalog-download flows

## Phase 2 - Production build

- Implement structured product and service content
- Add inquiry validation, spam protection, analytics, and consent handling
- Add SEO metadata, structured data, sitemap, and social previews
- Add automated tests, accessibility checks, and performance budgets

## Phase 3 - Launch and evidence

- Configure CI/CD, preview environments, monitoring, and backups
- Validate redirects, forms, analytics, and business contact paths
- Publish performance and accessibility results
- Document architecture, content operations, and deployment recovery
